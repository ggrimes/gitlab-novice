---
title: Learner Profiles
---

This lesson is written for researchers and analysts at the University of
Edinburgh and partner NHS organisations who work with data and code, but who
have not used version control before. It is usually taught as the final
workshop of a two-week Introduction to Data Science programme, in a hybrid
setting with some learners in the room and some online.

The profiles below describe the kinds of learners we design for. They are
composites, not real people.

## Aisha: postdoctoral research scientist

Aisha is a postdoc in a clinical research group, analysing patient cohort data
in R. She has used RStudio for several years but rarely opens a terminal and is
nervous about "breaking something" on the command line. She keeps old versions
of her analysis as `analysis_v2_final_FINAL.R` and shares scripts with
collaborators by email. She is joining online from her office.

Aisha wants a reliable record of how her results were produced, for papers and
for the next person to join the project. The RStudio Git episode and a steady
pace through the command-line steps will help her most, along with clear help
channels for online learners.

## Tomasz: PhD student in biological sciences

Tomasz is in the second year of a PhD and analyses sequencing data with a mix
of shell scripts and Python on the University HPC cluster. He is comfortable in
the terminal and has cloned repositories from GitHub, but he has never made a
commit or worked with a remote of his own. English is his second language.

Tomasz wants to put his analysis code under version control before his thesis
and to collaborate with a lab mate. He will move quickly through the early
episodes, so the challenges and the branching episode give him something to
stretch towards. Plain, consistent terminology helps him follow along.

## Priya: postgraduate taught student

Priya is on a one-year MSc and is new to programming. She has completed the
earlier workshops in the data science programme, so she has seen the shell and
R for the first time in the past fortnight. She is attending in person.

Priya needs to use GitLab for a group project later in her course. She benefits
most from the step-by-step local workflow, frequent short challenges that check
her understanding, and having a helper nearby when an error message appears.

## What these profiles mean for the lesson

- **Mixed prior experience:** challenges have worked solutions, and later
  episodes (Exploring History, Branching and Tagging) can be shortened or
  extended depending on the group.
- **Command line and GUI:** the RStudio episode gives learners who are less
  confident in the terminal a second route to the same workflow.
- **Hybrid delivery:** setup instructions are sent in advance, and an online
  helper supports learners joining remotely.
- **Institutional accounts:** setup uses University of Edinburgh GitLab, with
  gitlab.com as an alternative for learners without a University login.
